03 / CORE IDEAS核心概念

SIX IDEAS THAT DRIVE THE EXPERIMENT理解实验的六个关键概念

Follow the same sequence as the lesson.按课程顺序逐步建立理解。

01

PLACEMENT布局

Choose coordinates for movable cells while fixed pads remain where they are.为可移动单元选择坐标，同时保持固定焊盘不动。

A(0,0) · D(8,8) · fixed
02

HPWL半周长线长

Wrap a net in its smallest axis-aligned rectangle, then add width and height.用最小轴对齐矩形包围网络，再计算宽加高。

HPWL = W + H
03

SPRING ENERGY弹簧能量

Quadratic objectives use squared connection length, producing a smooth, linear derivative.二次目标使用连接长度的平方，使目标平滑且导数为线性。

E ∝ d²
04

NEIGHBOR AVERAGE邻居平均

The teaching implementation moves each free cell toward its neighbors' average.教学实现让每个自由单元向邻居平均位置移动。

new = 0.55 × avg + 0.45 × old
05

CONSTRAINED RESULT受约束结果

A and D keep most bounding boxes wide, so the starter improves only from 52 to about 48.A、D 使多数包围盒保持较大跨度，因此初始值仅从 52 降至约 48。

52 → 48 · same pads
06

DENSITY GATE密度门槛

A low HPWL can still hide overlap or density overflow. Check legality and density.低 HPWL 仍可能掩盖重叠或密度溢出，必须继续检查合法性与密度。

HPWL ≠ sign-off
